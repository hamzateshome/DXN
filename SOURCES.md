## Decisions

- Treat this as an independent distributor marketing website, not DXN's official national site.
- Use owner-supplied public contact information and code.
- Use factual ingredients/formats and link to the source catalogue. Do not repeat disease-treatment, immunity, detox, or guaranteed benefit claims.
- Do not import another country's retail prices or promise local availability.
- Do not manufacture a tracking URL from the distributor code. Registration and retail ordering are different journeys.
- Enquiry links open WhatsApp but never automatically send messages or create purchases.
- No founder/management portrait, fabricated testimonial, unsupported certification badge, or numerical business claim is used.
- Keep brand image copyright notices and watermarks intact.

## Outstanding business details

The owner has not supplied a verified DXN personal shopping URL, delivery terms, prices, or physical pickup location. The site directs visitors to ask Hamza personally rather than inventing these facts.
